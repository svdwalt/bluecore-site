What we do

BlueCore designs and validates network access control. We work out who and what is allowed onto a network, how each device proves who it is, and which policy it lands in once it is authenticated.

The tools are Cisco ISE, RADIUS, 802.1X, EAP-TLS and MAB. Every design is built in a lab, tested against real authentication flows, and documented so the evidence stands up when someone asks why it works.

Services

ISE design and deployment

Policy sets, identity sources, certificate profiles and authorisation results laid out before anything is clicked, then built and verified.

ISE health check and audit

Read-only review of a running deployment: version and support status, policy sprawl, certificate expiry, node health and what the logs say.

802.1X and EAP-TLS rollout

Machine and user certificates, PKI integration, OCSP and CRL checking, and a wired and wireless rollout order that does not lock anyone out.

MAB and IoT onboarding

Getting printers, cameras, controllers and other devices that cannot do certificates onto the network with the least privilege that still works.

Vendor-neutral RADIUS policy

A policy model written once in plain roles, then mapped onto Cisco ISE, Aruba ClearPass or Huawei so the same design ports between platforms.

Troubleshooting and migration

Failed authentications traced end to end, and controlled moves between policy servers or versions with a rollback path at every step.
